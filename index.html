About

A Little About Me

I am an aspiring Product Designer with a strong interest in UX/UI and graphic design. I am drawn to design because it turns real problems into clear, usable solutions that make everyday life easier.

I enjoy understanding a problem first: who is affected, what they need and where the experience breaks down. From there I explore ideas and shape them through research, wireframes, layouts and prototypes into designs that are simple to use and pleasant to look at.

My design experience comes from building complete projects across different areas: a UX/UI concept for finding electricians (PowerConnect), a brand identity for Tree Lodge at Sikumi, an editorial magazine, luxury skincare packaging, and poster and social media designs. Each one has strengthened my skills in user-centred design, visual communication, typography and layout.
